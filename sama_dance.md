# 🌀 How to Do Sama (Sufi Whirling) Properly

This comes from the tradition associated with Jalal ad-Din Rumi and the Mevlevi order.

## 1. Preparation (this part matters more than spinning)

* Stand still for a minute or two
* Slow your breathing (in through nose, out through mouth)
* Set an intention (not performance—something like *“I dissolve into the moment”*)

This aligns your mind before your body moves.

---

## 2. Posture

* Head slightly tilted to the right
* Right palm facing upward (receiving)
* Left palm facing downward (grounding)
* Spine straight, shoulders relaxed

Symbolically: receiving from above, giving to the earth.

---

## 3. The Turn

* Start slowly, pushing off gently with your right foot
* Turn counterclockwise
* Keep your left foot as the pivot, right foot stepping around
* Don’t fight dizziness—*soften your focus* instead of trying to “fix” it

The trick is not controlling the spin, but *surrendering into it*.

---

## 4. Breathing + Awareness

* Breathe naturally, don’t hold your breath
* Let thoughts pass—don’t chase them
* Focus on a feeling rather than an idea (like expansion or emptiness)

---

## 5. Duration

* Beginners: 1–3 minutes
* With practice: 10–20+ minutes
* Always stop gradually—don’t suddenly collapse

---

## 6. Closing

* Stop slowly
* Sit or lie down
* Let your body recalibrate

This “landing phase” is essential—otherwise you’ll feel disoriented.

---

## ⚠️ Important Reality Check

Doing this while high:

* Can intensify the experience, yes
* But reduces control, balance, and awareness
* In traditional practice, the altered state comes from the movement itself—not substances

So just be careful. There’s a difference between *deepening consciousness* and *overstimulating your nervous system*.
